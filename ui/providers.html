How Thoughtful works with providers

Thoughtful pairs licensed therapists with T, an AI companion that members use between appointments. Members get short, structured sessions and their therapist can see what they chose to share. This page explains what a provider goes through when they join.

What members see

A member chooses a provider from their matches. Each provider appears as a profile with a headshot, a personal quote, and a short bio. Members book time with a provider inside the app.

Joining as a provider

Providers are invited by the team. Once invited, setup happens in five steps.

1. Build your provider profile

Add your name, headshot, bio, personal quote, licensed states, and time zone. Your changes save automatically. When you submit, the team reviews and approves the profile before it reaches any members.

2. Set your availability

Set the hours you can offer members each week. Your primary calendar is connected automatically, and you can add more calendars. Members see a time only when it is free on your hours and on every calendar you connect.

3. Complete your training

Work through the training material, then mark it complete. You can come back to it any time.

4. Practice appointment

A member of the team reaches out to schedule your practice appointment. You will get an email from Cal.com with the time. Please attend without a reminder from us.

5. Go live

Once the team sets you live, your profile is in the Thoughtful app and your first bookable date is shown. Download the app and try a few sessions with T, and check your availability. Press Go live to finish setup and open the provider portal.
